## Design Considerations

The prototype has been developed using Microsoft Copilot Studio.

Further evaluation should consider:

- Integration with existing HR and ICT processes.
- Data protection and information security.
- Access permissions and appropriate controls.
- Human review and approval of agent-generated outputs.
- Operational support and maintainability.
